Where my mind goes · Etched & the opportunity

How I'd approach my first 60 days at Etched, a team that sees itself as the Manhattan Project meets Bell Labs.

Read me first

I know a hire like this is a two-way door. I lead a team of about six content strategists, with a high velocity of creative pitching and a lot of time spent helping them bring their stories to life. So I understand how this works: hire slow, fire fast if the fit isn't right. This plan is how I'd earn the fit.

The first 60 days · priorities
Roadmap · 13 Nov 2026 to mid-Jan 2027, and beyond
P1 · Lead

The owned documentary

A standalone documentary hosted by Sachin & Adam, living on Etched's owned channels. Crew is Tom, a DP and me as Etched's New Media hire, with a local Taiwan DP for the factory days. It's the softest launching pad.

My proposal: arrive a week early, around 13 Nov 2026, to get familiar with the team and spaces, do the site recce and prep mise-en-scène before Day 1.

  • Day 1 · 20 Nov 2026 · Etched HQ, San Jose. Sachin & Adam arrive and tour, founder sit-down #1, data centre walkthrough with Bryan, office B-roll before the rebrand.
  • Day 2 · Feb 2027 · Milpitas data centre. Racks going live.
  • Day 3 · Feb 2027 · HQ. Engineers, culture, interviews with young people who moved to San Jose.
  • Days 4–5 · Mar 2027 · Taiwan factory. Chips off the line, Sachin & Adam inside, a TSMC sit-down if possible.
  • Day 6 · late Mar / Apr 2027 · HQ. Second founder sit-down, the hard questions, closing interviews.
  • Day 7 · the next big launch. Optional.

My role is internal stakeholder: liaise with Tom and the DP, keep run and flow going, and work on mise-en-scène for the founder scenes.

P2 · React

The rebrand

A rebrand is coming. I stay reactive to comms tweaks and messaging off the back of it, so everything we ship speaks with the new voice from day one.

P3 · Standby

Two to three major announcements

Before the end of the year, possibly including a raise. I'm on standby for integrated campaign support, ready to answer:

  • What does an integrated campaign look like here?
  • What's the PR push?
  • Who are the preferred partners?
  • Are KOLs worth exploring, or antithetical to us?
  • What do events look like?
  • What does simultaneous social coverage look like?
