# What this site takes from the 1987 Apple HIG / Mac desktop, and what it leaves out

## Taken
- **Menu bar.** A single white bar fixed at the top with a black rule under it. Menus open on click and invert to white-on-black when selected, as in the HIG. The leftmost menu ("Calv") stands in for the Apple menu and holds About This Site, X, LinkedIn and Instagram.
- **Pull-down menus.** Each is a white box with a 2px border and a hard drop shadow. Items invert on hover, and dotted separators group related items. File is how you move around (Board, Timeline, Signals, Reading, Vouched for me, Beyond work).
- **Document windows.** Every card is a window: 2px black frame, square corners, and a 3px offset shadow with no blur.
- **Title bars.** Horizontal pinstripes with the title centred on a white knockout. Openable windows get a close box in the top left.
- **About box and dialogs.** About This Site and the expanded cards open as centred modal windows with a close box, like the Finder's About box.
- **Desktop pattern.** The desk is a 1-bit dot dither and the boot screen a 50% checkerboard grey, both made from 1px black-and-white tiles.
- **1-bit palette.** Black and white only. The "grey" is dither or plain grey text, never a colour.
- **Bitmap type.** Silkscreen for titles and menus (a Chicago stand-in), VT323 for system text such as the boot log and timeline years, and a Geneva/monospace stack for body copy.
- **Feedback.** Selection is shown by inversion, an outline or a thicker stroke, never colour, and hover states are instant.
- **Boot screen.** A white box on the grey pattern, with a small mark and a startup log, nods to the Happy Mac start-up.
- **Direct manipulation, plain language.** Click a thing to open it, short labels, no jargon. This follows the HIG's "see and point" and "consistency" principles.

## Left out on purpose
- **Draggable, overlapping windows.** They suit a desktop, not a scrolling page on a phone. Windows sit in a grid instead.
- **Zoom and grow boxes, scroll bars, window stacking.** They'd be decoration with no job to do.
- **Real Chicago/Geneva bitmap fonts.** Apple owns them, so free look-alikes are used.
- **Icons on a desktop (Trash, disk, folders).** Too much costume. It would turn the site into a Mac emulator and bury the content.
- **Apple logo.** Trademark, and not his. "Calv" takes its place.
- **Alert sounds, modal-only flows, keyboard Command shortcuts.** Not needed for a read-only site.
- **Strict 72dpi pixel sizes and the 512×342 screen.** The layout is responsive and modern-sized; only the look is 1987.
- **Faithful WhatsApp/X/LinkedIn chrome.** Embeds use the platforms' own widgets. Tom's message is a simple 1-bit speech bubble, not a copy of WhatsApp.
