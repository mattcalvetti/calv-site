The first 60 days

Calv@0xcalv · Draft plan, 2026
Read me first

I know a hire like this is a two-way door. I lead a team of about six content strategists, with a high velocity of creative pitching and a lot of time spent helping them bring their stories to life. So I understand how this works: hire slow, fire fast if the fit isn’t right. This plan is how I’d earn the fit.

Priorities

Three things, in order. The first is the softest launching pad; the other two are where I stay ready.

P1 · The owned documentary

LeadA standalone documentary hosted by the podcast hosts, living on the company’s owned channels. A five-day embedded shoot: HQ, founder interviews, the data centre, and a couple of days at the factory in Taiwan. I’m across the itinerary.

My role is internal stakeholder:

  • Liaise with the external producer and the DP so the thing gets done.
  • Keep run and flow going across the five days.
  • Recce the spaces we intend to shoot before we get there.
  • Work with the producer on mise-en-scène for the founder scenes.
P2 · The rebrand

ReactA rebrand is coming. I stay reactive to comms tweaks and messaging off the back of it, so everything we ship speaks with the new voice from day one.

P3 · Two to three major announcements

StandbyTwo to three major announcements before the end of the year, possibly including a raise. I’m on standby for integrated campaign support, ready to answer:

  • What does an integrated campaign actually look like here?
  • What’s the PR push?
  • Who are the preferred partners?
  • Are KOLs worth exploring, or antithetical to us?
  • What do events look like?
  • What does simultaneous social coverage look like?
